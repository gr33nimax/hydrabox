# HydraBox brand assets

The canonical HydraBox mark is the three-headed green halftone hydra on a
white rounded-square field.

## Master asset

- `assets/branding/hydrabox-logo.png` is the unmodified 1254 × 1254 source.
- Platform launcher icons are deterministic size derivatives of that file.
- Maskable and Android adaptive variants add only a white safety field; they
  do not redraw or recolor the mark.

Do not recolor, trace, crop, or replace the mark with the former blue
hexagon/tree placeholder. Keep sufficient white space around it when a host
platform applies its own icon mask.
